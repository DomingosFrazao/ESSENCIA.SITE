Nature of the practices

ESSEN.CIA practices are not clinical and do not replace medical or psychological care. Before more intense practices, we check whether they are suitable for you.

Session terms

  • Booking: sessions are booked by e-mail, WhatsApp or through the website form. A booking is confirmed once you receive written confirmation.
  • Payment: prices depend on the type of session and are given when you book. Payment is made before the session, by bank transfer or MB WAY. We issue an invoice with the tax number you provide.
  • Cancellation: the refund depends on the type of session and how far in advance you cancel.

  • Late arrival: the session ends at the scheduled time, even if it starts later because the participant is late.
  • Before the first session: we ask you for some information about your health, so we can choose suitable practices. Please tell us if anything changes.
  • When more intense practices are not suitable: pregnancy, epilepsy, cardiovascular disease, uncontrolled high blood pressure, recent stroke or surgery, glaucoma or retinal detachment, aneurysm, severe asthma, and some mental health conditions (for example, episodes of psychosis or bipolar disorder). In these cases, we adapt the practice or ask you to speak to your doctor first.
  • During the session: you can adapt the exercises, rest or stop at any time. Participation is voluntary and your own responsibility.
  • Confidentiality: what you share in a session stays between us. In group sessions, we ask all participants for the same care.
  • Photos and recordings: we do not photograph or record sessions without your agreement.
  • Minors: only with written permission from whoever holds parental responsibility.
  • Online sessions: you need a quiet place where you can lie down safely, and a stable internet connection.

Data protection

The controller of your data is Proporção Integra Unipessoal, Lda (contact details above). When you use the contact form or ask to receive news, your data is sent to our e-mail and recorded in a Google spreadsheet.

  • What data: name, e-mail and phone; the reason for contacting us and your message; your request to receive news, if you tick that option; billing details, if you book a session; and the health information you give us before sessions.
  • Purpose and legal basis: replying to you and preparing sessions (your request); sending news about sessions and events (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time); invoicing (legal obligation).
  • Health information: we only ask for it to make sure the practices are safe for you, with your explicit consent. It is not used for anything else or shared.
  • How long: contacts from the website and the self-assessment, 12 months after our last contact, or less if you ask us to delete them; if you are a client, for as long as that relationship lasts; the mailing list, until you unsubscribe (your subscription only becomes active after you confirm it in the e-mail we send you); billing details, for the period required by tax law.
  • Sharing: your data is never sold. We only use it with services needed to run the business: Brevo (sending e-mails and managing the mailing list) and Google (the spreadsheet where contacts are recorded, and e-mail).
  • Your rights: you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, or leave the mailing list, by e-mailing geral@essenciabreath.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNPD (the Portuguese data protection authority).

Breathing self-assessment

The self-assessment (in Portuguese only) keeps your answers and measurements in your browser only. Age, sex, height and weight are not sent. We only receive data if you leave your e-mail or phone and tick the matching option: your name, contact details and choices and, if you ask for the report by e-mail, a summary of your result, only to send it to you. For this we use Brevo (e-mail and list management) and a Google spreadsheet, where it is recorded. You can ask us to delete this data at any time by e-mailing geral@essenciabreath.com.
