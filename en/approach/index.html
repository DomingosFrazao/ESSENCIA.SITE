How we work

The essence of what we do

Each session starts by observing how you breathe, what you feel in your body and how you react to everyday situations.

We use breathing exercises, movement and attention to sensations, with time to integrate what comes up, on your own or by sharing. We choose the practices according to what you are looking for, your experience and your limits.

The work is organised in three dimensions: Breathe, Feel and Explore. They can be combined. They are not compulsory stages or separate services.

01
Breathing and regulation

BREATHE

How do you breathe under pressure? And when you want to rest?

We look at your breathing habits and try simple exercises to adjust your breathing comfortably.

These practices can help you handle pressure, stay focused and return to calm after demanding moments.

There is no single way of breathing that suits everyone. What matters is understanding how you breathe and what is useful to you.

02
Body and emotions

FEEL

We don't always notice what we feel in the body. We can spend the whole day with tense shoulders, a clenched jaw or held breath.

Through breathing and movement, we recognise these responses and see how they connect to emotions.

We can work with posture, grounding, free or guided movement and emotional expression. Each person decides how far to go.

There is no need to provoke emotions. You only share what you want to.

03
Awareness and integration

EXPLORE

Sometimes we want to understand our reactions better, the thoughts that keep repeating or the way we deal with difficult emotions.

We use more active breathing and movement practices, such as connected breathing or bioenergetics, which help go beyond the mind's usual control.

Broader states of awareness, strong emotions or experiences with personal or spiritual meaning may come up. We don't try to provoke them. At the end, there is time to feel and integrate them.

We choose the practices according to your readiness and what is suitable for you.

Integration

From the session into daily life

A session is a space to try things out and observe how you breathe, how your body reacts and how you feel.

The aim is to take that attention into daily life: noticing tension earlier, shifts in your state and the way you react to stress.

This change doesn't happen in one session. It builds up with practice and continuity.

Come and try it.
