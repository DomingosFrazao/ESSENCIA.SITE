The facilitator

Who is behind ESSEN.CIA?

I'm Domingos Frazão. I lead breathwork sessions and body awareness practices. I am also the founder of ESSEN.CIA and the person responsible for it.

My background

For more than 30 years I worked in technology, management, business development and project coordination. Years of decisions, responsibility and working with people in demanding settings. I saw first-hand how we live with pressure and how little room we leave to notice what is going on inside us.

Alongside that, I followed my own path through movement, music, sound and several personal development trainings. Little by little, breathing and body awareness moved to the centre.

What shaped me most was practice: support teams at retreats, work with somatic practices and bioenergetics, and many hours listening to and accompanying what comes up in one-to-one and group sessions.

Training: Breathwork Facilitator Training (INJOY) · Advanced Instructor Certification (Oxygen Advantage) · Working With People

How I work

Today, at ESSEN.CIA, I bring these experiences together in a simple, practical way. I lead sessions of conscious breathing, movement and body exploration, adapted to each person and their situation.

Sometimes the starting point is concrete: getting to know your breathing, handling pressure, gaining focus, recovering energy or slowing down. Other times, the session makes room for emotional expression, self-knowledge or deeper experiences.

I don't believe in one technique for everyone, or in an experience with an outcome fixed in advance. My role is not to interpret what each person goes through or to show them a path. It is to be present, offer tools when they make sense and create the conditions for each person to explore and integrate what matters to them.

Collaborations

I am part of the INJOY team, where I support retreats and sessions.

ESSEN.CIA's in-person sessions currently take place at the SEED studio, where I am also part of the facilitator team.

I'm also open to working with other professionals and organisations that work with breathing and the body responsibly.
