Breathing

Why we start with the breath

You breathe thousands of times a day. You hardly ever notice how.

Under pressure, the body responds. Muscles tighten, attention narrows and breathing tends to become faster and shallower.

Breathing reflects how we feel. It can also change how we feel.

Breathing happens automatically, but we can change its pace on purpose. That pace affects the autonomic nervous system, which helps the body speed up or slow down.

Breathing slowly and comfortably can help lower the body's activation and bring back calm and focus.

It is not about getting rid of stress or controlling emotions. It is about recognising the body's signals, being more aware of your breathing and having more ways to respond.

We start with the breath because it is the simplest way into the body. From there, we work with movement, sensations and emotions.
